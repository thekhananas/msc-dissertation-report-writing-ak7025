\section{External-Model Study Preliminary Findings}

In the initial sealed benchmark run, executable evidence showed a substantial apparent advantage over dialogue alone,
increasing correct predictions from 13/23 to 20/23 (a nominal 30.43 percentage-point gain).
However, the paired interval was wide and the exact McNemar comparison gave $p=0.0923$.
The verification protocol requires auditing execution traces to ensure string and sequence comparison validity.
