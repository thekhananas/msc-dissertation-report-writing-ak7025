\section*{Ethics and Data}

The work uses authored programming cases, model responses, controlled simulations and existing CSEDM learner records.
No student teaching intervention or measurement of human learning was conducted. Independent reviewers assessed evaluation
materials. Their contribution was a review of the research instrument.

A future study with students would require institutional review and agreed arrangements for consent, withdrawal, data handling,
accessibility and participant support.

The historical analysis uses the CSEDM 2019 Data Challenge records obtained through
\href{https://pslcdatashop.web.cmu.edu/DatasetInfo?datasetId=2865}{DataShop} \parencite{csedm2019challenge}. The report presents
aggregate results. It does not redistribute the learner-level source records.
