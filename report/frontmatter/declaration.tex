\section*{Use of Generative AI}

I confirm that this work is primarily my own and that no content generated by AI is presented as my original work. I acknowledge the use of OpenAI ChatGPT-5.6 (\href{https://chat.openai.com}{https://chat.openai.com}) and Anthropic Claude Sonnet 5 (\href{https://claude.ai}{https://claude.ai}) to assist with literature discovery, suggest fixes for code errors, recommend figure layouts, and identify areas for language revision and LaTeX formatting. I manually evaluated all AI suggestions, made all final edits to the text and code, and personally read and verified every cited source to ensure they are real and accurate.

Generative AI also served as an experimental tool. As described in the methods section, I used the \texttt{gpt-oss-120b} model through Cerebras to generate evaluation responses. These generated responses form part of the research data.

\section*{Ethics and Data}

The work uses authored programming cases, model responses, controlled simulations and existing CSEDM learner records.
No student teaching intervention or measurement of human learning was conducted. Independent reviewers assessed evaluation
materials. Their contribution was a review of the research instrument.

A future study with students would require institutional review and agreed arrangements for consent, withdrawal, data handling,
accessibility and participant support.

The historical analysis uses the CSEDM 2019 Data Challenge records obtained through
\href{https://pslcdatashop.web.cmu.edu/DatasetInfo?datasetId=2865}{DataShop} \parencite{csedm2019challenge}. The report presents
aggregate results. It does not redistribute the learner-level source records.

\section*{Sustainability}

The external-model experiment used a fixed set of prompts and retained the resulting responses. Later checks and analyses
replayed those records offline instead of making repeated provider requests or rerunning sandbox executions. The controlled
simulation used bounded scenarios and fixed evaluation settings. No large model was trained for this project. These choices
reduced additional compute during development and analysis. Energy use and carbon emissions were not measured, so this report
does not claim a quantified environmental impact.
